# Delivery roadmap

## Milestone 1 — Core foundation
- Registration/login
- Household creation
- Family member create/edit/deactivate
- Accounts
- Income and expense transactions
- Monthly summary dashboard
- Responsive PWA shell

## Milestone 2 — Complete finance engine
- Transaction edit and audit diff
- Categories and category analytics
- Account balances and transfers
- Monthly budgets and savings goals
- Loans, lending and repayment ledger
- Recurring bill scheduler and reminders

## Milestone 3 — Documents and offline
- Private receipt upload
- OCR extraction with review/confirm step
- PDF/Excel reports
- IndexedDB offline outbox and sync
- Duplicate/conflict review

## Milestone 4 — Production hardening
- Email verification and password reset
- HttpOnly refresh sessions
- Rate limiting and abuse protection
- PostgreSQL RLS
- Backup/restore drills
- Unit, integration, E2E and load testing
- Monitoring and error reporting
